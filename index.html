Xavier

Xavier is a personal AI assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps the owner with everyday work such as reading and updating spreadsheets, drafting documents, managing a calendar and organizing files in Google Workspace.

Who can use it

Xavier is a private, single-owner application. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no hosted backend and no other users. Only the account owner authorizes it, and only to act on the owner's own Google data.

How it works

  • The owner signs in with Google once and approves the requested permissions.
  • The resulting access token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Xavier uses the permissions only for tasks the owner asks for.
